About

True Axis captures real-world sites, structures, and parts with laser scanning and turns that data into accurate CAD and BIM models, then builds custom metal parts to fit. We work with contractors, manufacturers, and motorsport teams.

Services

  • 3D laser scanning
  • Scan-to-CAD and BIM modeling
  • As-built documentation
  • Reverse engineering
  • Custom fabrication: welding, CNC laser cutting, sheet metal
